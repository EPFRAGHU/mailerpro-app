How to get a Gmail App Password

Gmail requires an App Password (a 16-character code) instead of your regular password to send automated SMTP emails securely.

  1. Go to your Google Account Security Settings.
  2. Make sure 2-Step Verification is enabled under "How you sign in to Google".
  3. Search for "App passwords" in the top search bar (or go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords).
  4. Type an app name (e.g. Mailler App) and click Create.
  5. Copy the 16-letter code generated and paste it into the Password / App Password field in Mailler!
Tip: You can also use free SMTP providers like Brevo (300 free emails/day) or Resend without App Password setup!

How to get your Free Brevo SMTP Key

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) provides 300 FREE emails every day with great inbox deliverability and requires no 2FA app password hassle.

  1. Create a free account at brevo.com.
  2. In your Brevo dashboard, click your profile name at the top right and select SMTP & API.
  3. Click the SMTP tab and click Generate a new SMTP key.
  4. Give it a name (e.g. Mailler App) and copy the generated Key starting with xsmtpsib-....
  5. Paste your Brevo Account Email into "Email / SMTP Username" and your SMTP Key into "Password / App Password".
